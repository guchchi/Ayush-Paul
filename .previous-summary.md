## Summary of Changes — Shared Personalization Content System, Phase 1 Foundation

### What was built

A deterministic content personalization pipeline that transforms any (service, market, niche) triple into human-grade examples, helper text, and empty-state guidance — without LLM calls. The system spans 3 data files, 2 resolver modules, and a validation harness.

**Data layer** (`src/data/personalization/`):

- **`service-content-profiles.ts`** — 15 role-based service profiles (5 editors, 5 developers, 5 designers) + 3 track fallbacks. Each profile defines work nouns/verbs, execution/output terms, evidence language, helper text concepts, empty-state actions, example subject patterns, and avoid rules. Profile example patterns made distinct per service to minimize cross-service overlap.

- **`canonical-market-modifiers.ts`** — 17 market modifiers (YouTube Creators, Coaches, Agencies, etc.) with buyer questions, concern themes, trust expectations, language tendencies, CTA intent tendencies, and example framing influence.

- **`niche-semantic-metadata.ts`** — 3-tier resolution:
  - Tier A: 25 `AUTHORED_OVERRIDES` for high-priority niches (fitness_coaches, real_estate_agents, gaming_youtubers, etc.)
  - Tier B: 10 `TAG_RULES` for semantic composition from niche label keywords
  - Tier C: `deriveFromServiceMarket()` + `fallbackNicheModifier()` for unhandled niches

**Library layer** (`src/lib/personalization/`):

- **`types.ts`** — `PersonalizationContext`, `PersonalizationContextM1/M2/M3`, `ServiceContentProfile`, `NicheSemanticMetadata`, `CanonicalMarketModifier`, `PersonalizedContentBase`
- **`context.ts`** — `resolveM1Context`, `resolveM2Context`, `resolveM3Context`, `resolvePersonalizationContext`, `getServiceLabel`, `getCategory`, `getTrack`
- **`resolver.ts`** — `composeExamples` (with variantKey rotation for cross-service differentiation), `composeHelperText`, `composeEmptyStateGuidance`, `composePersonalizedStepContent`, `resolveServiceNiche`

**Validation** (`scripts/validate-personalized-content.ts`):

- Validates all 75 composite paths (15 services × 5 markets each)
- Audits service profiles, market modifiers, niche metadata
- Fabricated claims detector (excludes avoidClaims from check)
- Duplication gate: max example overlap below 70% threshold
- Generates 15 representative samples for visual inspection
