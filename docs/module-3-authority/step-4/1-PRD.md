# 📋 PRD (Product Requirements Document) - Module 3 Step 4: Authority Pack

## Purpose
The Authority Pack (Step 4 of Module 3) serves as the final compilation vault for the user's Authority System. It aggregates their chosen Authority Archetype, Proof Asset requirements, and Profile & Portfolio Strategy into a single, unified view. Its primary goal is to transition the user from "strategic planning" to "actionable execution" by providing exportable strategies and an interactive publishing checklist.

## User Problems
- **Fragmented Data:** After completing three intensive planning steps, the user's strategy is spread across different screens. They need a single source of truth.
- **Execution Paralysis:** Users often don't know what to do next after planning. They need a step-by-step checklist to actually build and publish their proof.
- **Portability:** Users need to take their strategy out of the web app (e.g., into Notion, Google Docs, or share with a VA) to execute it properly.

## Success Metrics
- **Export/Copy Rate:** High frequency of users clicking "Copy Full Strategy" or "Export Markdown".
- **Checklist Engagement:** Users interact with and complete items on the Build/Assemble/Publish checklist.
- **Progression Rate:** Users confidently advance to the next system (Portfolio System - Module 4) after reviewing their pack.

## Inputs & Outputs
- **Inputs:** 
  - Module 1/2 Context (Positioning, Offer details)
  - `authorityPosition`, `coreTrustPromise` (from Step 1)
  - `proofPriorities`, `proofAssets` (from Step 2)
  - `profilePortfolioStrategy` (from Step 3)
- **Outputs:** 
  - Tabbed visual summary of the complete strategy.
  - Downloadable `.md` file containing the compiled "Authority Pack".
  - Interactive, persistent checklist for execution tracking.

## AI Responsibilities
- Generate contextual, personalized empty state guidance based on the user's Module 1 and 2 inputs if they arrive at Step 4 prematurely.
- Dynamically format the exported Markdown to ensure high readability and logical flow based on the specific assets generated by the AI in previous steps.

## Edge Cases
- **Skipped Previous Steps:** User navigates directly to Step 4 without completing Step 3. (Solution: Render a prominent, personalized warning state urging them to complete Step 3).
- **Stale Context:** User returns to Step 1/2/3 and modifies their strategy after viewing Step 4. (Solution: The Vault and Checklist must automatically reflect the latest state, but completed checklist items should be preserved unless the underlying asset was deleted).
- **Empty Clipboard/Export Errors:** Browser blocks clipboard access or file download. (Solution: Graceful error handling with visible UI feedback).
