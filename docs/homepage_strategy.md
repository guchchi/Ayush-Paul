# HOMEPAGE STRATEGY

## PURPOSE

The homepage is not a directory.

The homepage is not a navigation page.

The homepage is not a project showcase.

The homepage exists to communicate:

1. What this ecosystem is
2. Why it exists
3. Why people should trust it
4. How people can participate

## SECTION GOALS

### HERO

Answer:

What is this?

Who is it for?

Why does it matter?

Do not explain everything.

Create curiosity.

---

### PROOF

Show evidence.

Use:

* Awards
* Competitions
* Projects shipped
* Impact metrics

Purpose:

Build trust before asking for attention.

---

### LEADERSHIP

Introduce founder.

Do not create a resume section.

Show:

Mission
Vision
Reason for building

Not skills.

---

### ECOSYSTEM

Most important section.

Show:

Who participates
What happens
How value flows

Visitors must understand:

Student → Learn → Build

Collaborator → Contribute → Create

Sponsor → Support → Impact

---

### CURRENT OPPORTUNITIES

Show:

What is active now.

Use:

* Cohorts
* Internships
* Collaborations
* Research

Create urgency.

---

### FEATURED WORK

Only showcase work that proves ecosystem outcomes.

Do not create a project gallery.

Every item must answer:

What happened?

Who benefited?

Why it matters?

---

### CTA

Role based.

Student

Sponsor

Collaborator

Each must have a different action.
