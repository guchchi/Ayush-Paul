# CONTENT PHILOSOPHY

## RULE 1

Show.

Do not tell.

Bad:

"Building the future."

Good:

"3 robotics systems deployed."

---

## RULE 2

Outcomes over activities.

Bad:

"We are experimenting."

Good:

"Created an autonomous navigation prototype."

---

## RULE 3

Evidence over claims.

Every claim must be supported by:

* Metrics
* Results
* Awards
* Projects
* Testimonials

---

## RULE 4

Visitors care about themselves.

Every section must answer:

Why should I care?

---

## RULE 5

Every page must have a purpose.

About:
Build trust.

Blog:
Teach.

Projects:
Prove execution.

Collaborate:
Create partnerships.

Momentum:
Show progress.

---

## RULE 6

Avoid founder worship.

The ecosystem must feel bigger than one person.

Show:

People
Projects
Programs
Impact

Not only Ayush.

---

## RULE 7

Clarity beats creativity.

If visitors do not understand it in 5 seconds,
the content has failed.
