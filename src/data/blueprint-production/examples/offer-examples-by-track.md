# Offer Examples by Track

## Editor

**Bad:** "I edit videos."
**Good:** "I turn your raw footage into scroll-stopping Instagram Reels that get 2x more views in 48 hours."

**Bad:** "I'm a freelance video editor."
**Good:** "I help content creators publish consistently by editing their talking head videos into polished, engaging content."

## Developer

**Bad:** "I build websites."
**Good:** "I build landing pages for early-stage startups that convert visitors into paying customers."

**Bad:** "I do web development."
**Good:** "I help small businesses get their first website online in 5 days — no code knowledge needed from you."

## Designer

**Bad:** "I make logos."
**Good:** "I design brand identities that make new businesses look established from day one."

**Bad:** "I'm a graphic designer."
**Good:** "I help coaches and consultants create social media graphics that actually look professional."
