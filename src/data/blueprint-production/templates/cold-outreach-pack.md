# Cold Outreach Template Pack

## Template 1: The Observation (Highest Reply Rate)

> Hey [Name],
>
> I've been following your [specific work/project]. Really impressed with [specific compliment].
>
> One thing I noticed: [specific observation about a problem or opportunity].
>
> I've helped [similar client type] with [specific outcome]. Happy to share how if you're interested.
>
> — [Your Name]

## Template 2: The Compliment + Curiosity

> Hey [Name],
>
> Your [specific piece of work] caught my eye — especially [specific detail].
>
> Quick question: do you handle [specific task] yourself or work with someone?
>
> — [Your Name]

## Template 3: The Value First

> Hey [Name],
>
> Saw your [post/work]. Had a thought that might help:
>
> [Specific insight or suggestion related to their work]
>
> No pitch — just wanted to share. Happy to dive deeper if useful.
>
> — [Your Name]

## Follow-Up Sequence

**Follow-Up 1 (3 days later):**
> Hey [Name] — just circling back. No worries if timing isn't right. Wanted to leave this here in case it's useful later.

**Follow-Up 2 (7 days later):**
> Hey [Name] — last message from me. Saw you're crushing it with [recent work]. If you ever need a hand with [service], I'm here.

## Tips

- Keep each message under 80 words
- Reference something specific from their profile
- Never pitch in the first message — start a conversation
- Send 5 messages per day minimum
- Track everything in your prospect tracker
