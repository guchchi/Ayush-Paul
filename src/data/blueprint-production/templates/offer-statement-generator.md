# Offer Statement Generator

## Formula

```
I [do X service] for [specific client] so they can [get Y outcome] in [Z time].

No-risk: [Your risk reversal].
```

## Template

```
I [action verb] [your service] for [target client type] so they can [specific outcome] in [timeframe].

No-risk: [guarantee or risk reversal].
```

## Examples by Track

**Editor:** I edit raw footage into scroll-stopping social clips for creators so they can 2x their engagement in 48 hours. No-risk: Free revision round if you're not happy.

**Developer:** I build landing pages for early-stage startups so they can convert visitors into leads within 5 business days. No-risk: Full refund if the page doesn't launch on time.

**Designer:** I design brand kits for new businesses so they can look established from day one. Delivered in 3 days. No-risk: Unlimited revisions until you love it.

**Writer:** I write LinkedIn content for founders so they can build authority and attract inbound leads in 30 days. No-risk: First post is free so you can see the quality.

**Marketer:** I set up lead generation systems for local businesses so they can get 10+ qualified leads per week. No-risk: Month-to-month, cancel anytime.

**Teacher:** I tutor students in [subject] so they can improve by one grade level in 4 sessions. No-risk: First session free — no commitment.
