# Phase 6 Checklist — Close Client

- [ ] Transition conversation from value to proposal (ask: "want me to put together a scope?")
- [ ] Present scope and price clearly (use the proposal template)
- [ ] Handle any objections (use the objection cheatsheet)
- [ ] Send agreement for signature (simple one-page is fine)
- [ ] Send invoice and receive payment (50% upfront recommended)
- [ ] Confirm start date and next steps
- [ ] Celebrate — you got your first client!
