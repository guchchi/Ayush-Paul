# Phase 5 Checklist — Outreach

- [ ] Write your 3-sentence outreach template
- [ ] Personalize your message for first 5 prospects (specific reference to their work)
- [ ] Send outreach to 5 prospects
- [ ] Track replies in your prospect tracker
- [ ] Send follow-up to non-responders after 3 days
- [ ] When someone replies, move to a call or DM conversation
- [ ] Aim for 5+ active conversations before moving to Phase 6
