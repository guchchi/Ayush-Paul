# Phase 2 Checklist — Create Offer

- [ ] Write your one-line offer statement (I [do X] for [client] so they can [outcome])
- [ ] Add a specific outcome metric (e.g., "2x engagement", "10 leads/week")
- [ ] Define your risk reversal (revision cap, satisfaction guarantee, etc.)
- [ ] Set your starting price
- [ ] Test your offer on 3 people and refine based on feedback
- [ ] Put your offer in your LinkedIn bio, portfolio, and first DM template
