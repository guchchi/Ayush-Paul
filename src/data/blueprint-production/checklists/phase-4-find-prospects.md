# Phase 4 Checklist — Find Prospects

- [ ] Pick 2 prospect source platforms from your track definition
- [ ] Create a prospect tracker (spreadsheet with columns: Name, URL, Need, Contact, Status)
- [ ] Search for signals: job posts, pain mentions, requests, hiring
- [ ] Add 20 qualified prospects to your tracker
- [ ] Research each prospect — what specific need do they have?
- [ ] Find the right contact method for each prospect
- [ ] Rank prospects by likelihood to convert (1-5)
