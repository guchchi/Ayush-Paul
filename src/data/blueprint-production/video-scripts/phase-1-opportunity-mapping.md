# Video Script — Phase 1: Opportunity Mapping

**Duration:** 3-5 minutes
**Title:** Why Most Freelancers Never Get Clients

---

**Hook (0:00-0:30)**

Most freelancers fail before they even start. Not because they lack talent. Not because they don't work hard. They fail because they try to sell everything to everyone.

"I edit videos." "I build websites." "I design logos."

Those aren't services. Those are categories. Clients don't buy categories. They buy specific solutions from specific people.

**The Problem (0:30-1:00)**

When you say "I edit videos," you're competing with every video editor on the planet. When you say "I help health coaches turn their raw footage into scroll-stopping Reels," you're the only one.

The difference between invisible and in-demand isn't skill. It's positioning.

**The Strategy (1:00-2:00)**

In this phase, we're going to map your opportunity. That means getting specific about four things:

1. Your service — exactly what you deliver
2. Your market — the broad category you operate in
3. Your niche — the specific segment you serve
4. Your positioning — one sentence that makes the right prospects say "that's me"

This is called Opportunity Mapping. Most freelancers skip this step entirely. That's why they struggle.

**The System (2:00-3:30)**

Five steps. Each one builds on the last.

Step 1: Pick your track. Video editor, designer, or developer. One only.

Step 2: Define your service. Not "video editing" but "Instagram Reel editing for creators." Get painful specific.

Step 3: Identify your market. Content creators. Early-stage startups. Small businesses. Name the broader world you serve.

Step 4: Select your niche. Narrower than your market. Health coaches who post daily Reels. SaaS founders launching a landing page. Coaches building their first brand.

Step 5: Write your positioning statement. One sentence that combines everything. This becomes your headline on LinkedIn, your bio on Instagram, your intro in every outreach message.

**Your Output (3:30-4:15)**

By the end of this phase, you'll have a completed Opportunity Profile. That's a single document that contains:

- Your service
- Your market
- Your niche
- Your positioning statement

This document is the foundation of your entire client acquisition system. Every future phase — offers, authority, outreach, closing — builds on this.

**Track-Specific Examples (4:15-4:45)**

An editor's positioning might be: "I help health coaches get consistent engagement on Instagram by editing raw footage into scroll-stopping Reels."

A developer's: "I help SaaS founders launch faster by building conversion-optimized landing pages in 5 days."

A designer's: "I help coaches look established from day one by designing complete brand kits in 3 days."

Same structure. Different tracks. Pick yours.

**Close (4:45-5:00)**

Most freelancers never do this work. They stay vague and wonder why nobody hires them. You're different. You're building a system. And it starts with knowing exactly what you offer and who you offer it to.

Open the Opportunity Profile Canvas. Let's map your opportunity.
