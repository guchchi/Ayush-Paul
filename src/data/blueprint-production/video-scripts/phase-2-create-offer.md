# Video Script — Phase 2: Create Offer

**Duration:** 3-5 minutes
**Title:** Skills vs. Outcomes — Why Clients Pay for Results

---

**Hook (0:00-0:30)**

Nobody wakes up and thinks "I need to hire a video editor." They wake up and think "I need more views on my content" or "I'm spending too much time editing."

Clients don't buy skills. They buy outcomes.

**The Problem (0:30-1:00)**

Most freelancers lead with their skill: "I edit videos." That's like a chef saying "I chop vegetables." Of course you do. But what do you produce?

Your skill is the input. The outcome is what the client actually pays for.

**The Strategy (1:00-2:00)**

We're going to package your service as an outcome-driven offer. The formula is simple:

I [do X] for [specific client] so they can [get Y outcome] in [Z time]. No-risk: [risk reversal].

Let me show you what this looks like for each track.

**Examples (2:00-3:00)**

Editor: "I edit raw footage into scroll-stopping social clips that get 2x more engagement in 48 hours."

Developer: "I build landing pages that convert visitors into leads in 5 business days."

Designer: "I design brand kits that make your business look established in 3 days."

Writer: "I write LinkedIn posts that position you as an authority and bring inbound leads."

Marketer: "I set up lead gen systems that bring 10+ qualified leads per week."

Teacher: "I help students master [subject] in 4 sessions."

**The System (3:00-4:00)**

Three steps:
1. Identify the outcome your service produces
2. Write your one-line offer using the formula
3. Add a risk reversal to remove hesitation

**Your Output (4:00-4:30)**

By the end of this phase, you have a single offer statement you can put in your bio, your portfolio, and your first outreach message.

**Next Phase Tease (4:30-5:00)**

Now that you have an offer, you need proof you can deliver. That's Phase 3.
