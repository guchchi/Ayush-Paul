# Prompt: Offer Creation

## Instructions

Copy and paste this prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI tool.

---

Act as a direct-response copywriter. I am a freelance **[track]** offering **[service]** to **[target client]**.

Help me create a one-line offer statement that makes prospects want to buy.

My service: **[describe what you do]**
My target client: **[describe who you help]**

Use this formula:
I [action] [service] for [client] so they can [outcome] in [timeframe]. No-risk: [risk reversal].

Give me 3 variations of this offer statement. Each one should:
- Focus on the outcome, not the process
- Include a specific metric or timeframe
- End with a simple risk reversal

Then recommend the best one and explain why it works.
