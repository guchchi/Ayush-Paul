# Prompt: Cold Outreach

## Instructions

Copy and paste this prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI tool.

---

Act as a cold outreach specialist. I need to send personalized messages to prospects to start conversations and get my first clients.

My track: **[track]**
My service: **[service]**
My target client: **[target client]**

The prospect I'm reaching out to: **[prospect name/description]**
Specific observation about their work: **[specific detail you noticed]**

Help me write a 3-sentence outreach message that:
1. Opens with a specific compliment or observation
2. Shows I understand their situation
3. Ends with a low-friction question or offer

The message should feel helpful, not salesy. Give me 2 variations.
