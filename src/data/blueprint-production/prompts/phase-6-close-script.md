# Prompt: Close Script

## Instructions

Copy and paste this prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI tool.

---

Act as a sales closer for freelancers. I'm in a conversation with a prospect who seems interested in my service. I need to transition from conversation to close.

My track: **[track]**
My service: **[service]**
My offer statement: **[your offer]**
My price: **[your price]**

What the prospect has told me so far: **[summarize the conversation]**

Help me:

1. Write a transition message that moves from conversation to proposal
2. Outline the scope I should propose (specific deliverables)
3. Suggest how to handle the 3 most common objections for my track:
   - "I need to think about it"
   - "That's more than I expected"
   - "Can you do it for less?"
4. Write the message where I ask for the commitment

Keep it natural and conversational.
