# Phase 1 Prompts — Opportunity Mapping

---

## Prompt 1: Opportunity Mapping

Copy and paste this into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI tool.

---

Act as a freelance business strategist. I need to map my opportunity so I can get my first paying clients.

My track: **[Video Editor / Designer / Developer]**

I can do: **[list skills you have]**

I'd like to help: **[describe the type of person or business you want to work with]**

Ask me targeted questions to help me define:
1. The specific service I should offer (narrow, not broad)
2. The market I should operate in
3. The niche within that market where I have the best chance of winning
4. My positioning statement

For each recommendation, explain why it's a strong choice for someone starting out. Push me to be more specific. A vague answer won't help me get clients.

End with a completed positioning statement I can use immediately.

## Prompt 2: Positioning Statement Generator

Copy and paste this into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI tool.

---

Act as a direct-response copywriter. I need a single positioning statement that clearly communicates who I help and what I deliver.

My track: **[Video Editor / Designer / Developer]**
My service: **[specific service you define]**
My target client: **[who they are]**
The outcome they get: **[what changes for them]**

Generate 5 positioning statement options using this structure:

"I help [target client] in [market] achieve [outcome] by [service]."

For each option:
- Use different language — one direct, one benefit-focused, one niche-specific, one problem-aware, one outcome-heavy
- Keep each under 20 words
- Make it something a prospect would read and think "that's me"

Then recommend the best one and explain exactly why it works for a beginner freelancer.

## Prompt 3: Niche Selector

Copy and paste this into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI tool.

---

Act as a market positioning expert. I'm a freelance **[Video Editor / Designer / Developer]** trying to choose a niche.

My broad market is: **[your market]**
My service is: **[your service]**

I'm considering these niche options:
- **[niche option 1]**
- **[niche option 2]**
- **[niche option 3]**

Evaluate each niche on:
1. **Need level** — How urgently do they need my service? (1-10)
2. **Ability to pay** — Can they afford my rate? (1-10)
3. **Accessibility** — Can I find and reach 20 of them? (1-10)
4. **Differentiation** — Can I stand out in this niche? (1-10)
5. **Scalability** — Can this niche generate repeat business? (1-10)

Score each niche and recommend the best one. Explain why it scores highest and what my first step should be to enter that niche.
