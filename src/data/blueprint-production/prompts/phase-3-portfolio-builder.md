# Prompt: Portfolio Builder

## Instructions

Copy and paste this prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI tool.

---

Act as a portfolio strategist. I am a freelance **[track]** trying to get my first 3 clients. I need 3 pieces of proof that show prospects I can deliver.

My track: **[track]**
My service: **[service]**
My target client: **[target client]**

I currently have: [describe any existing work, even if it's personal projects, practice work, or work for friends]

Help me create a portfolio-building plan:

1. What 3 types of proof would be most convincing for my track?
2. For each, describe exactly what I should create or collect
3. What platform should I showcase each on?
4. What should I write as a caption/description for each piece?

Focus on quality over quantity. I need 3 strong pieces, not 10 weak ones.
